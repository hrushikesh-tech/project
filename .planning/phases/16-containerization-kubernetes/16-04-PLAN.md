## Threat Model

<threat_model>

- **T-16-10: Verification drift** - If build/render commands are not discoverable and repeatable, later phases will treat unverified manifests as if they were production-ready.
- **T-16-11: Operator confusion at deploy time** - Without explicit runbooks, external dependency wiring and canary behavior become tribal knowledge and increase rollout risk.
- **T-16-12: False confidence from undocumented manual checks** - If live-cluster validations are omitted from the final evidence trail, CI/CD can inherit incorrect assumptions.

</threat_model>
