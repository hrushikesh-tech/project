## Threat Model

<threat_model>

- **T-07-13: Unverified request-path optimism** - Without integration coverage, the supply-chain APIs can look correct in unit tests while controller wiring, guards, or DTO behavior are broken.
- **T-07-14: Unproven reorder safety** - Reorder suppression and skip-reason persistence must be verified through worker-style flows, not only isolated method calls.
- **T-07-15: Validation drift** - If `07-VALIDATION.md` is not updated with real commands and statuses, execution-time sign-off remains ambiguous and easy to bypass.
- **T-07-16: Cross-phase regression blind spot** - Without end-to-end verification of the evolved receipt and PO flows, Phase 7 can accidentally break assumptions established by earlier AP/AR work.
  </threat_model>
