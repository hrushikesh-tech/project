# Plan 05-03: Leave Workflow, HR Background Jobs, Attendance Capture, and End-to-End Verification

<objective>
Implement the remainder of Phase 5: manager-first leave workflow with balance mutations and notification bridge, BullMQ-backed HR jobs for nightly accrual and stale-request auto-cancel, effective-date employee lifecycle transitions, and attendance capture/correction with derived overtime.
</objective>

<must_haves>

- Leave requests move only through legal DRAFT -> PENDING -> APPROVED / REJECTED -> CANCELLED transitions
- Approval deducts balance; qualifying cancellation restores balance; stale pending requests auto-cancel with a system reason
- HR jobs use explicit tenant scoping and are safe against duplicate processing
- Future-dated employee terminations finalize automatically at their effective date
- Attendance uses one record per employee per day with derived hours/overtime and no guessed clock-out
- Attendance corrections are limited to manager/HR paths and remain auditable
- Unit and integration coverage prove leave, attendance, and job behavior end to end
  </must_haves>

## Threat Model

<threat_model>

- **T-05-08: Illegal leave transition or balance corruption** - Leave requests must mutate status and balance atomically, with strict state guards.
- **T-05-09: Duplicate job execution** - Accrual, stale-request cleanup, and effective-date lifecycle jobs must remain idempotent and tenant-scoped.
- **T-05-10: Late-cancellation restoration bug** - Approved leave cancelled inside the 48-hour window must not restore balance or slip through as a soft cancel.
- **T-05-11: Attendance audit gap** - Corrections must preserve who changed the record, when, and why; the system must not guess missing times.
  </threat_model>
